About exam 500-770

This practice follows the Cisco course Advanced Secure Networking for Sales Professionals (ASNSP). The deck states the goal in sales language: understand advanced secure enterprise networking well enough to hold a confident conversation about Cisco solutions, use cases, and value. The same introduction names a recommended prior course, Secure Networking for Sales Professionals (SNESP), and expects basic networking literacy, core enterprise concepts, and the packet delivery process.

Cisco delivers 500-series exams through Pearson VUE, at a test center or with online proctoring where that exam offers it. On Cisco’s public price table, channel-partner sales-readiness exams in the 500 and 700 series are listed at $80 USD before tax, and specialist certification exams in the 500 series are listed at $300 USD before tax. A failed entry, associate, professional, specialist, or CCDE written exam has a five-calendar-day wait before a retake. Cisco’s public exam list does not publish a stable topic-weight blueprint for 500-770, and unofficial prep sites disagree on length, price, and outline. Confirm the live name, price, and blueprint in your Cisco certification account before you register.

The five course days in this deck are security concepts, WAN technologies, the routing security stack, the secure campus, and wireless. The live Pearson VUE exam is a separate, shorter event. These 200 items are study questions written from the slides. They are not copies of exam questions.
