About exam 500-770

The review document describes a proctored certification exam at a Pearson VUE test center: 90 minutes and 60 questions, or 120 minutes for candidates who are not native English speakers. It recommends sitting the exam within about two weeks of the course. The same document says this library is not that exam and does not test hands-on configuration.

Question weight in the library follows the course blueprint it cites, ASNSP Exam Blueprint v1.0.2: Security Foundations 20%, WAN/VPN and advanced routing 20%, routing security and secure campus 10%, campus fabrics and architectures 10%, and enterprise wireless 10%. The remaining 30% of the certification exam is the three-day Secure Networking Essentials course, which this five-day library does not cover. Most items are one best answer of four. Four items ask you to choose two of five.

Cisco’s public price table lists channel-partner sales-readiness exams in the 500 and 700 series at $80 USD before tax, and specialist exams in the 500 series at $300 USD before tax. Confirm the live exam name, price, and blueprint in your Cisco certification account before you register.
